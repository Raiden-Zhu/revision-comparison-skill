# Optional Academic Style Profile

Use this profile when proposing academic revisions. Follow the editing scope and fidelity rules in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md); this profile adds prose preferences, not permission to change scientific content. In compare mode, preserve the supplied versions exactly.

These preferences are adaptable. Follow the author's requested style and supplied voice samples rather than imposing one manuscript template.

1. **Preserve the author's voice.** Follow supplied examples of rhythm, transitions, terminology, and claim placement. Revise approved wording only to address a concrete problem.
2. **Write directly.** Use concrete subjects, actions, and findings. Remove empty promotion and performative defensiveness while retaining material caveats.
3. **Make formal ideas readable.** Prepare the reader for necessary notation and terminology. Add intuition or examples only when they resolve a specific reasoning gap.
4. **Remove repetition, not reasoning.** Keep the shortest explanation that preserves the reader's path through the argument.
5. **Connect neighboring passages.** Give each passage a clear role and check that references, transitions, and comparisons remain understandable in context.
6. **Match the register.** Follow the target manuscript's language and scholarly conventions. Use the requested language for comparison explanations.

Apply the relevant preferences during the writing step. Do not run a second rewrite merely to enforce this profile. Check the resulting passages in context, and explain concrete improvements rather than assigning a style score.
